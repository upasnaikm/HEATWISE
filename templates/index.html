🛡️ Local Heat-Resilience Action Matrix • Shital & Sajag Bharat

Evidence-informed civic and clinical preparedness protocol mapping candidate alert thresholds to municipal interventions.

🏛️ NDMA / Heat Action Plan Guidelines
Yellow Early Watch ~41.5°C

Trigger Condition: 80th percentile risk elevation or first summer inflection point.

  • ▸ ORS Kiosks: Activate oral rehydration points at transit hubs and anganwadi centers.
  • ▸ Labor Advisory: Issue hydration reminders to construction sites and agricultural cooperatives.
  • ▸ Early Triage: Alert primary health centers (PHCs) to stock oral electrolytes and ice packs.
Target Focus: Community Awareness & Vulnerable Population Hydration
Orange Alert ~43.5°C

Trigger Condition: 90th percentile moderate surge threshold.

  • ▸ Cooling Shelters: Open air-cooled municipal community halls, bus depots, and religious shrines.
  • ▸ Work Hours Shift: Mandatory work stoppage for outdoor manual laborers between 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM.
  • ▸ Emergency Ward Preps: Pre-fill ice packs and cold intravenous normal saline packs in casualty wards.
Target Focus: Exposure Reduction & Hospital Emergency Capacity
Red Severe Emergency ~45.0°C

Trigger Condition: 95th percentile acute surge crisis threshold.

  • ▸ Water Tanker Fleets: Deploy emergency drinking water tankers to high-density informal settlements.
  • ▸ Power Outage Moratorium: Prohibit scheduled utility power cuts to maintain domestic fans and cooling.
  • ▸ Inter-Agency Command: Activate District Magistrate (DM) emergency heat response cell and 108 ambulance redeployment.
Target Focus: Critical Life Support & Disaster Management Escalation
